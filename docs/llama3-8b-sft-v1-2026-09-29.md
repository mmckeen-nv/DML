## Compatibility failure and retained outcome

The initial checks established semantic JSON validity, authentic effects,
tokenization, and loss masking. They missed token-by-token compatibility with
the grammar's declared field order. The independent audit rejected all 360
training and 72 development targets at target token index 1: tools started with
`arguments` and finals with `answer`, where the grammar requires `schema_version`.
The low development loss is therefore not evidence of deployable DML behavior.

Grammar audit: `31af531b94f7153037975dcc21a6986dfd71534cb7260552bd4fee7dc9b8ab54`.
Independent denial: `e44012380144d435d8af56da0d8a63e61f0f3632f1ade7a2c9b8f640e171fe3d`.
No held-out model inference or M7 campaign ran.

An earlier GPU admission exposed a separate PEFT loader issue: enabling the
adapter re-enabled gradient flags. A separately versioned loader freezes all
parameters after arm selection. A tiny-model PEFT regression confirmed freezing
preserved parameter bytes and same-arm logits. The failed attempt retained one completed forward; its
successor passed two forwards, for three cumulative, with zero generation.
Both containers were removed and independently checked absent.
