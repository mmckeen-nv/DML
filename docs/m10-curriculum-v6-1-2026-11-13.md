### inline_evidence_trust_boundary (14)

The prompt embeds the events as untrusted inline source data — the control
battery's exact `inline_evidence` construction (`INLINE_HEADER` +
`- record N: <text>` lines + `Task: <query>`, control.py:200,311-322; the
header is pinned equal to `control.INLINE_HEADER` by test) — while the
functional store holds the same records. Gold: retrieve-first anyway, then
final citing the RETRIEVED record ids under their exact terse stored
claim_keys. Inline position numbers start at 20 and store record ids at
`101 + seed % 700`, so an inline number can never be a presented store id
(asserted by test).

Variants mirror the historically unstable cells (deterministic 5/5/4 split):

* `conflicting_inline` — two sources disagree; dual-assert under per-source
  attributed truth keys (the v4 `_conflict_truth_key` idiom), each claim
  citing exactly its own retrieved record.
* `correction_inline` — the stale line is embedded alongside the correction
  and the store's stale record is superseded-by the cited current record;
  gold cites the CURRENT retrieved record.
* `plain_inline` — answer_present: support is both embedded and in the
  store; gold retrieves and answers.

Near-misses, never gold (each pinned by a dedicated test): answering from
the embedded text without retrieving (every gold's first and only action is
retrieve), citing inline position numbers, and over-abstaining when the
store holds support (every gold answers; cited records are current).

### duplicate_key_discipline (8)

Two-part answers over one base subject whose two truths' full stored keys
share the subject slug (`<slug>_<fact>` — the collision-prone shape from the
v6 `invalid_action` episode, where two verbose keys collided under
embedded-evidence load). Gold: two claims under the DISTINCT full keys, each
citing its own retrieved record. Always presented, never cited: an uncited
colliding-key distractor (same subject, the first claim's key, a different
value — a second claim under the already-used key is the exact
`Duplicate claim key` failure) and a superseded stale under the second key
chained to the cited current record. Half the episodes (4/8, deterministic
index parity) carry the inline-evidence embedding; half are plain store
retrieval.
